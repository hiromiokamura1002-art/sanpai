3. Unofficial app

This is an unofficial study app for people preparing for the completion test of the training course required to apply for an industrial waste collection and transport business permit in Japan. It has no connection with the body that runs the course, the Ministry of the Environment, any prefecture, or any other public organization. The questions are original practice questions written from published laws and public documents; they are not actual test questions. Passing is not guaranteed. The content is based on the laws as of 2026-11-22 and may differ from current rules after amendments. The app is for exam study only; for actual permit applications and waste handling, follow the latest laws, the instructions given in the course, and the guidance of your prefecture or designated city.
